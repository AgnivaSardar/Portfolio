## 📸 Ready for Your Photo

To add your photo to the hero section:

1. Replace the placeholder image URL in `index.html` (line ~95):
   ```html
   <img src="your-photo-path.jpg" alt="Portfolio" class="hero-image">
   ```

2. The image will automatically:
   - Appear on the right side of hero section
   - Have the pop-out 3D effect
   - Float up and down smoothly
   - Have red border and glow

Recommended image specs:
- Dimensions: ~500x600px (portrait)
- Format: JPG or PNG
- Size: < 500KB for fast loading
